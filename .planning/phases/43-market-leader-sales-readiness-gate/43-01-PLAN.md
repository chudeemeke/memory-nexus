## Objective

Prove `@chude/memory` is excellent across architecture, security, quality, product readiness, competitive positioning, and Product North Star conformance, or leave the phase incomplete with explicit blockers.

## Threat Model

- False readiness claim: docs or reports imply market readiness while executable gates still fail.
- Silent feature loss: competitive gaps such as MCP/server or benchmark parity get ignored instead of owned.
- Privacy regression: readiness evidence or package smoke leaks real local paths, secrets, transcripts, or private config.
- Remote sync egress risk: conflict/recovery evidence fails to prove no push happens after a failed pull/rebase.
- Stale project truth: `.planning/PROJECT.md`, README, roadmap, or requirements disagree with actual implementation status.

Blocking severity: high. Any high-severity unresolved item blocks Phase 43 completion.
